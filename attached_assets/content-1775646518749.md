## Building Safe AI  From Amsterdam

I'm an AI alignment researcher based in Amsterdam, working at the intersection of machine learning safety and formal verification. As founder of The Witness Protocol and Realm101, I develop frameworks that ensure AI systems behave transparently and remain corrigible.

My research focuses on the critical challenge of our time: how do we build increasingly powerful AI systems that reliably serve human values? I approach this through interpretability research, decision-trace auditing, and formal safety specifications.

I believe alignment isn't just a technical problem—it's a design philosophy that must be embedded into every layer of AI development.

## AI Alignment Research

Four active areas of inquiry, each targeting a different dimension of the alignment problem.

01

### Witness Protocol Framework

Transparent AI Decision-Making

A comprehensive protocol for auditing AI behavioral patterns through interpretability logging and decision trace verification. Every model decision is recorded, analyzed, and verified against safety specifications.

Active Development

02

### Alignment Verification Methods

Formal Safety Proofs

Formal verification approaches that mathematically prove model outputs satisfy safety specifications. Combines constraint satisfaction solvers with reward modeling analysis.

Research Phase

03

### AI Risk Assessment Framework

Catastrophic Risk Evaluation

Systematic methodology for evaluating catastrophic risk scenarios in advanced AI systems. Provides quantitative scoring across capability evaluation, misuse potential, and containment strategies.

Published Framework

04

### Value Learning & Corrigibility

Human-Aligned AI Values

Research into robust mechanisms for AI systems to learn, internalize, and maintain human values. Includes corrigibility guarantees and preference learning from human demonstrations.
